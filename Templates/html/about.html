About Us

❂

The Bodhgaya Math is an ancient monastery belonging to the Giri order of Hindu Sannyasis. This order is one of the ten orders of the Dashanami Sampradaya, which was founded by Adi Shankaracharya himself. Therefore, the Bodhgaya Math, by virtue of being a Giri-order monastery, follows the spiritual and philosophical traditions laid down by Shankaracharya, primarily the Advaita Vedanta philosophy of non-dualism. The monastery was not founded directly by Adi Shankaracharya but rather in the late 16th century (around 1590 A.D.) by a Gosain (a type of ascetic) named Ghamandi Gir, a holy devotees of this order.

The Bodhgaya Math is situated on the bank of sacred river Nilanjana, in the midst of the garden extending over the area of about 52 bighas, and surrounded by a 10 feet high masonry wall. The huge building that we are seeing at present was built in 1811. There are two gates to enter the Math, one from the river side and the other from the Mahabodhi Vihar side. The entire management of Bodhgaya Math is done only by monks who dedicate their whole life to the Math.

Our History

𑁍
Pre-Independence History

The Bodhgaya Math is an ancient monastery belonging to the Giri order of Hindu Sannyasis. This order is one of the ten orders of the Dashanami Sampradaya, which was founded by Adi Shankaracharya himself. Therefore, the Bodhgaya Math, by virtue of being a Giri-order monastery, follows the spiritual and philosophical traditions laid down by Shankaracharya, primarily the Advaita Vedanta philosophy of non-dualism. The monastery was not founded directly by Adi Shankaracharya but rather in the late 16th century (around 1590 A.D.) by a Gosain (a type of ascetic) named Ghamandi Gir, a holy devotees of this order.

The Bodhgaya Math is situated on the bank of sacred river Nilanjana, in the midst of the garden extending over the area of about 52 bighas, and surrounded by a 10 feet high masonry wall. The huge building that we are seeing at present was built in 1811. There are two gates to enter the Math, one from the river side and the other from the Mahabodhi Vihar side. The entire management of Bodhgaya Math is done only by monks who dedicate their whole life to the Math. Many notable works have also been done by the Math and written documents are also available in this context like after the Battle of Plassey, the first Indian Independence Movement was carried out, which is also called Sanyasi Vidroh. When the British occupied Bihar, Bengal, Orissa, at that time Bodhgaya Math played a very important role in the rebellion. During this movement, many Mahatmas sacrificed their life, whose Samadhi is in the premises of Mahabodhi Temple. Over the centuries, the Bodhgaya Math gained immense power and wealth, becoming one of the largest landholders in the region and the Mahant took on the role of custodians of the Mahabodhi Temple and its surrounding area. Bodhgaya Math had more than 565 branches in villages and small Gurukuls were run in each of the states. Along with the religious education in the Gurukul, many works were done by Bodhgaya Math in all areas like water canals and drains were installed for irrigation in the area of Magadh. By the 18th century, it had become one of the largest state run by the monks, which is called Bodhgaya State which was extended to the areas of present day Hazaribagh, Ranchi and Chhattisgarh and Mahant Ji used to do farming on about 65000 acres of land, from Rajgir to Kakolat waterfall. Apart from the field of education, another notable work was done by the Math. In the 12th century, when Bakhtiyar Khilji destroyed the Bhagwati temple and the Mahabodhi temple, all the Buddhist monks and worshippers went to live in another place. During this period, the temple was managed by Bodhgaya Math, with Hindu rituals as the Buddha is revered as an incarnation of the Hindu god Vishnu. At that time, Mahabodhi Temple was managed by the Mahants of the Math and from time to time, Mahant ji has played an important role in the preservation of the temple. The Mahabodhi temple that we see today was once in the form of ruins and in 1875, the Mahabodhi temple was rescued by Mahant ji, the King of Burma and the East India Company. Until the formation of BTMC Act in 1949, the management of Mahabodhi Temple was done by Bodhgaya Math and even after the formation of BTMC Act, the management of the temple was done by the Mahant of the Math and the control of the temple was given to BTMC on 12 May 1953. One of the fact, the statue of Lord Buddha in Mahabodhi Temple was moved from Bodhgaya Math and the Pran-Pratishtha of the statue was done according to Hindu rituals by the Mahant of Bodhgaya Math. This has been mentioned in the Report of Buchanan made in 1895, which he had submitted to the District Judge. Also, it has been described by the English officials as well and the entire saga has been described in the report of Alexander Cunningham.

Post-Independence

Bodhgaya Math has done a lot of great work after independence In the field of education, when the state government needed to open a university in Magadh, Math had supported to establish Magadh University by donating 500 acres of its own land and when there was a problem in running the Gaya College that we see today, Math had donated two of its estates of Aurangabad to university, so that the college could be built with the tax received from these estates. Similarly, 30-40 schools or colleges like Harihar Sanskrit Vidyalaya, Shakmuni College, Homeopathic College, Suryabhatt High School, Ramsahay Giri Vidyalaya have been built by Bodhgaya Math. In the time of famine, the local poor residents were fed in the Math and Mahant Ji voluntarily donated land to many poor people. The history of Bodhgaya Math has been very glorious, when Vinoba Bhave was leading the Land Movement, gave 11111 acres of land to distribute among the poor. Along with this, excavation was done in the premises of Mahabodhi Temple under the chairmanship of Alexander Cunningham in which statues related to Hinduism and Buddhism were found and all those statues were kept in the Math. Later on, more than 100 statues were donated to the Patna Museum, some have been donated to the Bodh Gaya Museum and some are still present in the Bodhgaya Math. According to the agreement between Mahant Ji and the Government of Bihar, Bodhgaya Math will have independent rights over the temple and Mahant Ji will be a permanent member of Mahabodhi Temple. Bodhgaya Math has the right over all the idols of Mahabodhi's main temple - our three Samadhis, Mata Annapurna Devi's Temple, Vishnu's Feet, Five Pandava's Temple, Hari Gauri Ji, Shankar Ji, Ganesh Ji. Today, five priests of Bodhgaya Math are appointed there and according to the ancient tradition, even today Lord Buddha is worshipped by the devotees of the Math in the morning and evening, because in our culture we consider Lord Buddha to be the incarnation of Vishnu Ji.

Shivalaya

There are 20 Shivalayas built in the Math premises and the reason behind this is that at one time more than a thousand sanyasis used to live here, so that they do not face any inconvenience in worshiping.

Bara Darri

✧

Bodhgaya is the land of Lord Buddha, where Lord Buddha attained enlightenment. This happened before the establishment of the Math, Lord Buddha used to go to the well near the river just opposite the Mahabodhi Temple and used to meditate there and realized that he has to spend his entire life in this peace. According to his belief, Lord Brahma appeared there and explain to him that Lord Buddha has to spread this knowledge in the whole world. Keeping this incident in mind, two stupas have been built there by the king of Burma, which we call Brahma Stupa which is still in the Bodh Gaya Math premises.

Samadhi

⚘

During the Sanyasi Vidroh, many Mahatamas sacrificed their life and whose samadhi is in the premises of The Mahabodhi Temple. There are around 200 Samadhis of the Mahatma, every Mahatma who has completed their life, their body is buried in Samadhi area.

Akshay Patra

✺

Mata Annapurna is the goddess of our Math. In the 16th century, Mahant Mahadev Giri did severe penance in front of the Mahabodhi temple. Pleased with this, Mata Annapurna gave Akshaya Patra to the Math, which is still present here. The specialty of Akshaya Patra is that no metal can make grains to stick like a magnet, but if we take grains to Akshaya Patra, it sticks to it like a magnet. This is the special attraction and identity of our Math. Akshaya Patra has a special place in all the books written about Bodh Gaya Math.
